Session replay lets you inspect a recorded user session and see behavioral context that aggregate charts cannot show on their own. Use replays to investigate confusing flows, repeated interactions, navigation problems, and potential friction around conversion.

Replay is most effective when you first identify a pattern in analytics and then review representative sessions to understand what might explain it.

## Configure session replay

Open **Settings** from the Session replay page to control whether Trfyx records sessions and how much visitor traffic is included. [https://app.mintlify.com/trfyx/trfyx/~/4402ea60-5690-45ab-9ec2-5c684f0b2403](https://app.mintlify.com/trfyx/trfyx/&#x7E;/4402ea60-5690-45ab-9ec2-5c684f0b2403)

### Recording settings

The **Record sessions** toggle controls whether Trfyx captures visitor sessions for replay. Turn it on to collect recordings and turn it off to stop recording new sessions.

The **Sample rate** controls the share of visits that are recorded. For example, a value of **100%** means the recording configuration is applied to all visits that are eligible for session replay. Lower sampling rates reduce the number of recorded sessions while still giving you a representative sample of visitor behavior.

Use a lower sample rate when you have enough traffic that recording every visit is unnecessary. Use a higher rate when you need broader coverage while investigating a specific issue.

### Privacy settings

The **Privacy** section controls what visitor input is visible in recordings.

**Mask typed text** replaces typed values in the replay with `***` instead of showing the original text. Keep this enabled when you do not need to inspect the exact values visitors enter.

> **Important:** Privacy settings should be tested with realistic test data before you rely on them in production. Do not assume that a sensitive field is protected simply because session replay is enabled.

## When to use replay

Use session replay when you need to investigate questions such as:

- Why are users leaving a page without completing the primary action?
- Do people repeatedly click or retry an interaction?
- Where do users appear to hesitate in an onboarding flow?
- Did a UI change introduce a confusing navigation path?
- Do mobile visitors experience a different obstacle from desktop visitors?

A replay provides context for a specific session. It does not explain what every user experienced, so use it alongside aggregated reports and funnels.

## Review a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the behavior you are investigating">
    Start with a specific observation, such as a decline in signup completion or a page with unusually high exits. Note the time period and relevant audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find relevant recordings">
    Open the session replay area in Trfyx and use the available filters to narrow the recordings to the project, period, page, or user behavior relevant to your question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for observable friction">
    Look for repeated attempts, backtracking, unexpected navigation, stalled progress, or interactions that do not appear to produce the intended result. Separate what you directly observe from what you infer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare more than one session">
    Review multiple recordings, including sessions that completed the desired action when possible. One unusual session may be an edge case rather than a broad usability issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate the hypothesis">
    Check the relevant [funnel](/funnels), [goal](/goals), or product behavior. If you change the interface, compare outcomes after the change rather than relying only on a replay impression.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy and responsible use

Session recordings can contain information about people's behavior and may expose sensitive content if the implementation is not configured appropriately. The **Sample rate** and **Mask typed text** settings help you control how much data is recorded and how typed input appears in replays, but you should still verify the captured output with test data.

[https://app.mintlify.com/trfyx/trfyx/~/dbf3b438-24b0-473a-ab41-a9224b061fa0](https://app.mintlify.com/trfyx/trfyx/&#x7E;/dbf3b438-24b0-473a-ab41-a9224b061fa0)

Before enabling replay in production:

- Review your privacy notice, consent requirements, and internal data-handling policy.
- Use the masking, exclusion, and privacy controls available in your Trfyx setup, and verify their behavior with test data.
- Avoid collecting passwords, payment card details, authentication tokens, private messages, or other sensitive inputs.
- Limit access to recordings to people who need it for their work.
- Retain recordings only for as long as needed for the intended purpose and applicable obligations.

Do not assume sensitive fields are automatically masked. Test what is captured before collecting real user sessions.

## Common mistakes

**Treating one replay as representative.** Use a set of sessions and compare successful and unsuccessful paths.

**Assuming a click means success.** A recorded interaction does not necessarily mean that a server request succeeded or the intended business action completed. Validate the corresponding event or goal.

**Ignoring device differences.** Responsive layouts and mobile interaction patterns can be different. Compare like with like.

**Using replay without an outcome metric.** Define the problem in analytics, inspect the experience, and then validate the effect of a change.
